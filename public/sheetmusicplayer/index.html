How to use

  1. Open a score – Choose an image or a PDF, take a photo, or drop a file on the left panel. Any file can be chosen, but only images and PDFs can be read.
  2. Wait for the reading – The first time, the reading engine is downloaded (about 130 MB). Reading takes from about ten seconds to a minute per page, depending on your computer.
  3. Play – Press Play. A blue cursor moves along the staff with the music. Click a note to start from there.
  4. Tempo – The tempo starts at the marking found on the page (♩ = 120 when there is none). Change it with −5 / +5, the number, or the slider, even while playing.
  5. Fix mistakes – Switch to “Edit”. Each note is a box: drag it up or down to change the pitch, sideways to move it, drag its right edge to change the length, click an empty spot to add a note, and right-click to delete. Click a measure number to add or delete measures. Back in “Score”, the staff is redrawn with your changes.
  6. Save – “MusicXML” saves the result. You can also open it in MuseScore and most notation apps.

Privacy

  • Your music stays on your device – The photo, image, or PDF you open is read only in your browser and is never sent to a server. The photo you take with the camera is not saved. This app has no accounts, cookies, or ads.
  • Data saved in your browser – The last score (the notes the app read, as MusicXML), its file name, and the tempo are saved in your browser's storage so they are there next time. The app and its reading engine are saved there too, so they work offline. You can delete them by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Server logs and page views – This site is hosted on Cloudflare. Standard access logs (such as IP address, time, and the requested page or file) are kept for operation and security. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies (the page URL, referrer, browser information, and page load time).
